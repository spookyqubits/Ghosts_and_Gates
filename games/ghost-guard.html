How to play

Goal. The Keeper hides a ghost in the box: a row of three HEADS coins and a row of three TAILS coins, each with its own chips (the secret). The Guardian wins if after seven nights the ghost comes back exactly as it started. The Gremlin wins if it does not.

Hands. Guardian: 3 Check + 4 Rest. Gremlin: 2 Flip + 5 Calm (Level 2: one Calm becomes a Sign). Every night each player plays one card face-down.

CardWhat the Keeper does
CalmNothing.
Flip kTurns coin k over in both rows.
Sign kIn every row whose coin k shows TAILS, swaps every chip's colour.
RestNothing.
CheckSays only: "Coins 1 & 2 match / don't match. Coins 2 & 3 match / don't match." The answer is the same for both rows.
Peek kShows coin k, but the ghost must pick one row (chance ∝ chips²). The other row is lost, and so is the secret.

End. After night 7 there is one free final Check and Fix. Then the box opens. Same chips, same colours, same rows: the Guardian wins.

Level 2 is a diagnosis. The single Sign error cannot be repaired by the available Check and Flip cards. Try the short Sign + Check example, compare the chip colours with the secret, then explain what is missing. You can also explore all seven nights. Neither route adds a duel win or loss.

The physics. The two rows are the two parts of one qubit stored in three qubits (the 3-qubit bit-flip code). Chips are amplitudes, colours are signs. Flip is a bit-flip error (X), Sign is a phase error (Z), Check measures the parities Z₁Z₂ and Z₂Z₃, and Peek is a measurement that collapses the superposition. Two flips between checks make the Guardian "fix" the wrong coin and swap the rows: a logical error.
