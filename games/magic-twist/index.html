Model and graphene experiment

The large pattern is called a moiré. For two ideal, identical lattices rotated by an angle θ, its spacing is L = a / [2 sin(θ/2)], where a is the lattice pitch. For small angles, L is approximately a/θ when θ is in radians. At 1.10°, the period is about 52.1 dot pitches, or 10.42 cm in the 2 mm model.

In graphene, a is the 0.246 nm lattice constant. The same geometric estimate gives about 12.8 nm at 1.10°. Each printed or rendered dot stands for a lattice site in the simplified pattern. In a 2018 experiment near this angle, researchers measured zero resistance at low temperatures and certain carrier densities. The overlap shows periodic alignment.
