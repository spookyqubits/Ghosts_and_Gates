Catch the Photon wave cards

40 cards: seven 1s and three 2s per direction. Blue ↑, red ↓, purple →, gold ←. Print all seven sheets at 100%, cut around each border, and use identical opaque backs or sleeves.

Every plot uses the same amplitude scale and frequency. Opposite phases cancel completely only at equal strength. Arrows describe board moves, not the photon’s travel.
