How to play

Goal. The coin starts as one green chip on HEADS. Player A wins if the coin ends on HEADS. Player B wins if it ends on TAILS.

Turns. A, then B, then A each pick one card in secret. The cards stay face-down. Then reveal them and do the moves in order.

CardWhat happens to the chips
LeaveNothing.
FlipSwap what is in the two boxes.
SignEvery chip on TAILS changes color.
GhostEach HEADS chip becomes a same-color chip on HEADS and one on TAILS. Each TAILS chip becomes a same-color chip on HEADS and an opposite-color chip on TAILS.

Clean up. A green and a red chip in the same box are a zero pair: remove both. If two matching chips are left in a box, keep one.

Open the box. Chips in one box only: that side wins. Chips in both boxes: toss a real coin.

Levels. 1: only Leave and Flip. 2: Player A also gets Ghost. 3: Player B also gets Sign. 4: everyone gets every card.

On a projector: type a card's letter (L, F, S, G) instead of clicking, so nobody sees the choice.

Why can Player A always win in Level 2? (spoiler)

A plays Ghost: the coin is now in both boxes with the same colors. Flip just swaps two matching chips, so B's move changes nothing. A plays Ghost again: the two TAILS chips are one green and one red, a zero pair, so they cancel. Only HEADS is left.

In Level 3, B can play Sign. The coin still looks 50/50, but now the HEADS chips cancel after the second Ghost, and the coin lands on TAILS. A hidden sign you cannot "see" decides the game.

The chip rules are exact: they give the same results as the real gates, and the coin can only be HEADS, TAILS, or a 50/50 ghost. Level 2 is David Meyer's quantum penny-flip game (Physical Review Letters 82, 1052, 1999), where a quantum player always beats a classical one. Zero pairs connect to Common Core 7.NS.A.1a: opposite quantities combine to make 0.

Discussion questions. Is Level 1 fair? Why does A win every time in Level 2? The Sign card changes nothing you can see. Why does it matter? Does a ghost coin with different colors look different from one with the same colors when you open it?
