THE FIRST GAME IN SPOOKY SIGNALS

Ghost Mail.

How much of a secret will you give up to test it?

Alice sends a packet. Bob receives it. Eve might be listening. As the Courier, open a few envelopes to look for mismatches, then decide whether to deliver the rest. Every extra test costs a bit of your possible key.

Switch sides as the Spy: intercept a little or go all in. More listening can mean more points—and more chances to get caught.

The science behind the story

Ghost Mail simulates preparation and measurement in the Z and X bases, public basis comparison, and an intercept-and-resend attack on BB84. Noisy Night adds detector errors, so a mismatch has more than one possible cause.

The points are game rules, not a security test. The game does not create real encryption keys. Real quantum key distribution needs authenticated communication and additional processing. Read the model notes and sources ↗
