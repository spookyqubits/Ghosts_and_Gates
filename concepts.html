ψStates & measurement

Born rule

With a normalized state, square the magnitude of a basis outcome’s amplitude to find its probability.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchRead the chance of measuring 1, then use the Look block to sample an outcome.
  • Ghost CoinSigned wave chips set the odds of Heads and Tails.
  • Ghost HuntThe squared room amplitude gives its chance of being found.
  • Quantum DriftAdd the squared left and right amplitudes at each position.
  • Catch the PhotonSquared wave magnitude sets detector chances in the optical model.
  • Bloch SphereA state’s vertical coordinate sets its Z measurement odds.

Superposition

A quantum state can combine several basis states with amplitudes.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchApply Ghost to a fresh qubit to prepare an equal superposition of 0 and 1.
  • Ghost CoinThe Ghost card puts wave chips in both outcome boxes.
  • Ghost GridA move marks two squares in a classroom analogy.
  • Ghost HuntThe search begins with amplitude in every room.
  • Ghost ChoirThe oracle acts on a superposition of input strings.

Measurement bases

The basis specifies which set of outcomes a measurement can distinguish.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchPrepare a qubit, compare its odds in X, Y, and Z, then measure in the basis you choose.
  • Ghost MailCompare straight Z and diagonal X settings before reading a bit.
  • Ghost Copy ShopChoose X or Z to expose a machine’s copying limits.
  • Bloch SphereRelate the direction of a state to Z measurement odds.

Measurement & state update

A measurement produces an outcome and can change the state used to predict what comes next.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchMeasure a qubit twice and compare the two readings.
  • Schrödinger’s Cat Decay LabInspect the blocks to see how a detector measurement records the first decay.
  • Ghost CoinOpen the box to turn the displayed chances into one outcome.
  • Ghost GridWatch connected moves settle in a classroom analogy for collapse.
  • Ghost MailSee how measuring in the wrong basis can change a resent state.

Complex amplitudes & relative phase

Relative phase can change what happens when amplitudes combine.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchPut a phase gate between two Hadamard gates and see how the angle changes the measured odds.
  • Ghost NumbersPractice quarter-turns and multiplication on the complex plane.
  • Ghost CoinReverse a sign without changing the immediate 50/50 odds.
  • Bloch SphereWatch S and T rotate states around the Z axis.
  • Quantum DriftPreview the hidden sign change before later interference.
  • Catch the PhotonCombine wave arrows that point at different phases.

Bloch sphere

A point on the sphere represents a pure state of one qubit.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchRotate a qubit about X, Y, or Z and predict its measurement odds in each basis.
  • Bloch SphereRotate three qubit states and compare their endpoints.
  • Ghost RelayCompare the qubit’s state arrows before and after correction.

HGates & algorithms

Quantum gates

Gates transform amplitudes in a controlled way.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchBuild circuits with H, X, Z, phase and rotation gates, controlled-X, controlled-Z, SWAP, and Toffoli.
  • Ghost CoinFlip, Sign and Ghost act like X, Z and Hadamard.
  • Bloch SphereCompare X, Y, Z, H, S and T as rotations.
  • Ghost ChoirFinal Hadamard gates turn the oracle’s phase pattern into bits.
  • Energy ValleyAdjust Y rotations in a circuit that prepares two spins.

Quantum interference

Amplitudes add when they contribute to the same outcome.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchChange the slit spacing and measure the brightness at different points on the screen.
  • Catch the PhotonBuild or cancel the detector’s wave in Wave Lab.
  • Ghost CoinWatch opposite chips in one box cancel to zero.
  • Quantum DriftCombine paths arriving in the same direction channel.
  • Ghost HuntIncrease amplitude in the marked room through repeated steps.

Grover search

Alternating a marking operation and a reflection amplifies the target.

  • Ghost HuntFlip the marked room’s sign, reflect about the mean, and choose when to look.

Quantum walks

A coin operation mixes directions, then each direction shifts the walker.

  • Quantum DriftSteer the distribution and compare its spread with a classical walk.

Oracle query complexity

Count how many times an algorithm calls a hidden function.

  • Ghost ChoirCompare independent parity questions with one Bernstein–Vazirani query.
  • Ghost HuntCount marking calls as you choose the number of search rounds.

Variational energy minimization

Tune a circuit’s parameters to lower its expected energy.

  • Energy ValleyChoose settings from noisy readings and uncover the true energy landscape.

⟷Entanglement & Bell tests

Entangled states & Bell pairs

An entangled pure state cannot be factored into states of its parts.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchApply Ghost to the first of two fresh qubits, link it to the second, and compare their measurements.
  • Ghost RelayA shared Bell pair supplies the teleportation resource.
  • Ghost SwitchboardJoin two link pairs into a shared endpoint pair.
  • Energy ValleyCompare an unentangled preparation with the two-spin singlet.
  • Ghost Copy ShopTest a controlled flip that entangles its outputs for some inputs.
  • Haunted SquareThe ideal team’s strategy uses two shared Bell pairs.

CHSH game

With equally likely questions, shared entanglement can raise the win rate above the classical 3-in-4 bound.

  • Ghost TwinsCompare a fixed plan with the ideal entangled strategy over many rounds.

GHZ correlations

Three entangled qubits can satisfy a set of parity rules no fixed classical plan can satisfy.

  • Ghost TrioTest all four question cards against a fixed plan and the ideal GHZ model.

Mermin–Peres magic square game

Classical plans can satisfy eight of nine intersections. The ideal entangled strategy can satisfy all nine.

  • Haunted SquareBuild a classical plan and compare it with the simulated entangled team.

No-signalling

Entanglement alone cannot transmit a chosen message.

  • Ghost RelayExplore the receiver’s state before the two classical bits arrive.
  • Haunted SquareCompare matching joint answers with random local answers.

⇄Quantum communication

BB84 key distribution

Preparing and measuring in two bases lets a sample reveal disturbances.

  • Ghost MailKeep matching bases, test some bits, and weigh noise against interception.

Quantum teleportation

A shared pair, a joint measurement and two classical bits transfer a qubit state.

  • Ghost RelayReceive and read the two bits to choose X and Z corrections.
  • Ghost PostRepeat the transfer across five hops and check every correction.

Entanglement swapping

A joint measurement at a relay entangles the two distant endpoints.

  • Ghost SwitchboardBuild both links, swap at the relay, and send the endpoint label.

No-cloning theorem

No single operation can perfectly copy every unknown quantum state.

  • Ghost Copy ShopFind an input and measurement that expose each claimed copier.
  • Ghost RelayTrace what happens to the original state during a teleportation.

Loss, flags & classical records

A network can flag a lost link or state. Classical records can be copied and resent.

  • Ghost SwitchboardReact to failed link attempts and finite memory lifetimes.
  • Ghost PostBalance flagged quantum state loss against time and score.
  • Ghost RelaySpend stamps to recover missing or unreliable classical bits.

±Noise & error control

Bit flips & phase flips

X swaps the computational basis states. Z reverses their relative sign.

  • Ghost GuardCompare the visible effect of a bit flip with the hidden sign error.
  • Ghost CoinCompare the Flip and Sign cards before opening the coin.

Parity checks & error syndromes

Compare parts of an encoded state to learn about an error without reading the stored bit.

  • Ghost GuardUse two checks to locate one flip, then test where the checks fail.

Shot noise

An estimate from finitely many measurements varies from batch to batch.

  • Schrödinger’s Cat Decay LabRun batches of 100 nuclei and compare the measured decay rate with the prediction.
  • Energy ValleyCompare estimated energy with the exact value at three shot counts.
  • Ghost Copy ShopCompare 64 simulated outcomes with the machine’s exact probabilities.
  • Ghost TwinsCompare a four-round result with a larger randomized batch.

Coherence & measurement

Recording which path or direction was taken can remove interference between those alternatives.

  • Ghost Blocks for ScratchSwitch on the slit detectors and see how recording the path changes the interference pattern.
  • Quantum DriftRecord directions in the Observer lab and compare the resulting spread.

λNuclear physics

Radioactive decay & half-life

In each half-life, a surviving nucleus has a 50% chance of decaying.

  • Schrödinger’s Cat Decay LabChoose how many half-lives to wait and test the predicted fraction of nuclei that decay.

◇Quantum materials

Moiré geometry

Twisting two repeating lattices makes a new pattern with a larger period.

  • Magic TwistPredict the angle that gives a target spacing, then measure it.

Magic-angle twisted graphene

Near a particular twist angle, graphene layers can show unusual electronic behavior, including superconductivity under specific conditions.

  • Magic TwistModel the geometry near 1.1°. The activity discusses the electronic experiment as context.

Coupled spins & Hamiltonians

A Hamiltonian specifies the energy of a system and its interactions.

  • Energy ValleyUse the XX + YY + ZZ model to compare two-spin energies.
