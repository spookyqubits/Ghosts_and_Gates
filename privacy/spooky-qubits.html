SPOOKY QUBITS

Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Spooky Qubits is a collection of quantum-themed games. Its first game is Bloch Chase. This policy describes the Spooky Qubits Android app, version 0.1.0.

Game information stays on your device

The app saves your game mode, arena, and completed moves on your device so you can resume a round. Spooky Qubits does not collect, receive, or share that saved-game information. Shared-phone play does not send game information to another device.

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

The app does not require an account, include advertising or in-app purchases, or use analytics or tracking services. Its games work offline. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Deleting saved games

You can remove saved games by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. In the browser demo, clear the page's browser storage instead.

Google Play and Android

Google Play, Android, and services supplied by your device may process information under their own privacy policies. This policy describes the behavior of the Spooky Qubits app.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information to respond to your inquiry. Do not include passwords, payment information, or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact spookyqubits@gmail.com. We will update this page if the app's information practices change.
