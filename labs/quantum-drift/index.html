Rules and model

Each card changes the coin state at every position, then left and right directions shift one step along the line. Ghost maps (L,R) to ((L+R)/√2,(L−R)/√2); Flip swaps directions; Sign reverses R; Leave keeps the coin state and still shifts. Every player spends five cards per round.

The walker starts at zero pointing right. In round two you swap roles with the computer while the initial state stays the same. Right receives 100 × (P(right) + P(zero)/2) points, and Left receives the rest. LOOK samples a fresh preparation for illustration. Kempe, quantum random walks
