College lab questions

Compare two policies over several runs: attempt the slower link first, or take whichever link is open first. Record how many pairs expire and how many endpoint pairs you identify. Explain why either outcome depends on both memory lifetime and link success.

Model and experiment

The relay holds two separate qubits. Its joint Bell measurement consumes both neighboring pairs and prepares an endpoint pair with a label set by two classical bits. C uses those bits to identify the Bell state. Creating the connection prepares an entangled resource for a future protocol.

Here swapping and message delivery are ideal. The success rates, hard expiry, and time slots are classroom rules. Hermans et al., three-node experiment
