College lab questions

Find a test that lets the controlled copier pass and one that exposes it. Explain why copying orthogonal states in one basis is possible. For a |+⟩ input, compare the controlled copier’s joint X-basis distribution with measuring two independently prepared copies.

Model and experiment

Every run starts with a fresh source and target |0⟩. The controlled copier applies CNOT. The transfer device swaps the source and target. The measure-and-prepare device measures Z and uses the result to produce both outputs. The table compares probabilities for these devices with the probability for two independently prepared copies.

Unitary evolution is linear. A device that copies |0⟩ and |1⟩ sends their superposition to an entangled state, while two copies would be a product state. This audit illustrates a counterexample to the claim. Wootters and Zurek, no-cloning
