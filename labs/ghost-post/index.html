    Correction guide

    First bit: Z, a half-turn about the Z axis. Second bit: X, a half-turn about X. A 1 means apply it. A 0 means leave it off.

      College lab questions

      Before you run the comparison, guess how often a route with five fast links will reach the end. Compare it with a route that uses three fast links and two checked ones. How much time does the safer route cost you?

      Pick a hop in the history. Point to the shared pair the hop used up and the two bits it sent. Try a wrong correction on a fresh route and see whether the displayed state can still match. What does the score tell you that the match alone leaves open?

      Model and experiment

      Every hop uses one shared pair. The Bell measurement gives you two bits to send. Turn on Z when the first bit is 1 and X when the second bit is 1. The replay can show how close you got because the simulator still has a record of the starting state.

      A fast hop has a 5% chance of losing the state every time. Checked hops always survive in this game. Five fast hops in a row give a survival chance of 0.95⁵, about 77.4%, before you account for any wrong corrections. The game flags lost states. Real equipment can introduce other errors too.
