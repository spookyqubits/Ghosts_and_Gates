College lab questions

Multiply Alice’s nine entries by grouping them into rows. Then multiply Bob’s nine entries in columns. Why must their two plans differ somewhere? Find a pair of plans that agree at eight intersections and explain why observing nine lucky rounds is a separate question.

Model and experiment

For matching answers at the chosen intersection, the joint quantum distribution is uniform over the eight allowed pairs of triples. The corresponding commuting Pauli observables, row by row, are IX XI XX, ZI IZ ZZ, and ZX XZ YY. Measurements on two Bell pairs give agreeing signs at the shared cell.
