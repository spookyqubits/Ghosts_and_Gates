For educators.

Pick one game and play a turn together. Ask students what they think will happen, then let them test their guess. You can start with one round and save the quantum vocabulary for the discussion.

ZIP · Games, facilitator guides, and printables

Pick a game

In Ghost Coin, ask which chips will cancel. In Catch the Photon, try to send a detector back to zero. For a class that knows tic-tac-toe, try Ghost Grid and ask what happens when two pairs share a square.

Show the first move together

Project one turn before students play in pairs. Open Guide & downloads on the game’s card for the materials, timing, and printables.

Talk about what changed

Ask, “What did you expect? What happened?” Then pick one part of the game, such as chips canceling, and discuss what it helps explain about quantum behavior.
