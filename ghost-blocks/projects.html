Five quantum projects in Scratch

Open a project in Ghost Blocks, then click the green flag. The downloadable Scratch editions use Scratch’s own blocks to reproduce the same experiments.

Download all five Scratch editions

Phase Arcade

Turn the phase dial, predict which port will win, and watch 100 shots test your guess. Try 0°, 90°, and 180° to see a phase turn into a measurable difference.

Read about single-photon interference

Photon Studio

Paint two screens one photon at a time. Add batches and compare the double-slit pattern when paths are hidden or marked.

See the original controlled double-slit electron experiment

Bell Pair Lab

Compare an entangled pair with two shared random bits. Predict whether their results will match in the X basis after both preparations match in Z.

Schrödinger’s Cat Decay Lab

Choose how long a simulated nucleus waits before you check the detector. Compare the predicted decay rate with your tally after 100 trials.

Teleportation Lab

Send a qubit state. Read Alice’s two classical bits and choose which gates Bob needs to recover it. Try a different input state to test your rule.
